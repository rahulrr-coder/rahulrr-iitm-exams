Computational Thinking Roadmap

Iteration through graphs and recursion, weeks 1–9 (papers don't reach W10–12). Each week: learn its archetypes (solve the flagged target question with help, internalise the trigger), then the full ladder below with every row tagged to its archetype. Progress saves in this browser.

How to run this

Open a week. Read its archetype cards first — each names the pattern, the recognition trigger, and one target question to solve with AI help. Once that pattern clicks, work down the ladder table below: every row carries a small badge (e.g. W3-A2) linking it back to the archetype that solves it.

Mark an archetype “Learned” once its trigger feels automatic, not just after solving the target question once. Tick ladder rows as you clear them — both feed the streak and the dashboard's daily plan.

The roadmap

Archetypes are AI-generated clusters over the classified PYQ set, aimed at grouping questions by solving technique rather than by topic label — a judgement call, not an official grouping. Progress is stored per-browser (localStorage).
